### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Electric Cyan & Ice Blue vs Generic Neon Cyan**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Multi-stop gradient combining brilliant white, ice blue `#38BDF8`, and electric cyan `#00F0FF`.
  - *Why*: Prevents flat neon cartoonishness, producing a high-end luxury cyan sheen that matches Apple / Porsche / sci-fi cinema palettes.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Single-color flat cyan `#00FFFF`. Passed over because flat cyan looks cheap, whereas multi-stop gradient with white highlights looks ultra-expensive and sophisticated.

- **Decision 2: Cinematic Scrim Layout vs Pure Dark Void**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Real high-resolution cinematic photography integrated seamlessly beneath a dark contrast scrim.
  - *Why*: Eliminates the "empty dark screen" feeling and delivers immediate emotional impact and visual grandeur.
